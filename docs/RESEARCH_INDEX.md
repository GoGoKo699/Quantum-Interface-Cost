## Reading the evidence

The [scientific evidence freeze](SCIENTIFIC_EVIDENCE_FREEZE.md) pins the
selected proof package and its essential exact certificate. The
[reproducibility record](REPRODUCIBILITY.md) documents the wider archive's
checks. Analytical proofs, exact scalar certificates and fixed finite
matrix diagnostics have different roles; a passing diagnostic is not a
universal converse.

The teaching material introduces the selected results without requiring
this entire archive. General entropy and asymptotic-rate questions remain
future work, alongside the unrestricted common-accuracy problem beyond
the proved ranges.
