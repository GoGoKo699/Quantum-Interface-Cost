## 1. What this adds, and what is already prior

The evaluated profile C has an exact two-correlation interpretation:
with one trusted qubit, it is the least state entanglement of formation
consistent with the two correlations, without specifying marginals or
cross correlations. Dimension three on the other side suffices, with one
fixed readout pair for the entire profile. Dimension two instead has the
strictly larger raw cost throughout the noisy nonclassical interior.

The raw cost is a direct specialization of Verstraete–Wolf's sharp
CHSH/concurrence theorem. Convexification and the qualitative advantage
of orthogonal flags are also prior. The new application here is the
complete dimension comparison and explicit fixed qutrit realization of
the already evaluated C. This is not a new derivation of C's phase boundary
or a claim that these deductions establish historical priority.

The original interface contract is unchanged: one unknown specimen, one
delayed local X/Z query, arbitrary collective encoding, free finite
classical records, worst-case quantum dimension and uniform input/query
error. The following state-realization problem is a comparison. Its
finite dimension **includes the classical flag**; the original memory
cap does not. No qutrit lower bound for the original interface follows.

## 2. A precise dimension theorem

Let B be a trusted qubit with fixed Pauli X,Z. For d>=1 define

$$
E_d(x,z)=\min_{\substack{\omega_{AB}\text{ on }\mathbb C^d\otimes\mathbb C^2\\
 -I\le A_0,A_1\le I\\
 \operatorname{Tr}(\omega A_0\otimes X)=x\\
 \operatorname{Tr}(\omega A_1\otimes Z)=z}}
 E_F(\omega_{AB}),\qquad 0\le x,z\le1.
\tag{1}
$$

The observables represent arbitrary binary POVMs. They are fixed for the
chosen realization; any locally accessible preparation label controlling
them must be included in A's dimension. No additional external shared
flag is allowed in (1). Empty feasible sets have value infinity. No
assumption on omega_B or the unreported correlations is imposed.

Use the existing notation

$$
f(v)=h_2\!\left(\frac{1-\sqrt{1-v^2}}2\right),\qquad
\gamma(x,z)=f\!\left(\sqrt{[x^2+z^2-1]_+}\right).
\tag{2}
$$

Here C is the evaluated convex profile in
[PRODUCT_DIAGONAL_PROFILE_RATE.md](../PRODUCT_DIAGONAL_PROFILE_RATE.md),
generated by the free unit disk and the exact-axis atoms (1,v),(v,1)
with cost f(v).

**Theorem.**

$$
E_2(x,z)=\gamma(x,z),\qquad
E_d(x,z)=C(x,z)\quad(d\ge3).
\tag{3}
$$

One fixed qutrit pair attains the latter minimum throughout the square:

$$
A_0=1\oplus Z_A,\qquad A_1=1\oplus X_A.
\tag{4}
$$

Moreover

$$
E_2>E_3\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
x^2+z^2>1\ \text{and}\ \max(x,z)<1.
\tag{5}
$$

The least A dimension attaining the unrestricted minimum C is exactly
one on the compatibility disk, two on the nonclassical exact-axis edges,
and three everywhere else outside the disk.

### 2.1 The qubit lower bound is an old sharp theorem

Verstraete–Wolf, *Entanglement versus Bell violations and their behaviour
under local filtering operations*,
[quant-ph/0112012v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0112012v1), 3 December
2001, Eq. (2), p. 1, normalizes CHSH to local bound one. Theorem 1, p. 2,
gives `beta<=sqrt(1+c^2)` for every two-qubit state of concurrence c;
Eq. (1), p. 1, uses Wootters' E_F=f(c).

For any realization in (1), choose the auxiliary trusted observables
`B_+=cos(theta)X+sin(theta)Z` and
`B_-=cos(theta)X-sin(theta)Z`. Its normalized CHSH expectation is

$$
\tfrac12\langle A_0(B_++B_-)+A_1(B_+-B_-)\rangle
=\cos\theta\,x+\sin\theta\,z.
$$

Maximizing theta gives sqrt(x^2+z^2). The prior theorem consequently
forces `c>=sqrt([x^2+z^2-1]_+)`, proving E_2>=gamma.
General binary contractions do not evade the bound: their extreme points
on a qubit are Pauli reflections or ±I. When either observable is scalar,
the normalized CHSH operator above has norm at most one; nontrivial
reflections are covered by the prior theorem. Convexity in the two
observables completes this extension without a dimension-changing dilation.
The auxiliary settings are a mathematical use of trusted X,Z, not extra
measured data assumed in (1).

For attainment outside the disk, purify the qubit marginal with Bloch vector

$$
r=(\sqrt{1-z^2},0,\sqrt{1-x^2}).
$$

Its entropy is gamma. On its qubit purification, the separate optimal
binary contractions attain correlations
`F_X=sqrt(1-r_y^2-r_z^2)=x` and
`F_Z=sqrt(1-r_x^2-r_y^2)=z`. These identities follow by taking trace norms
of `sqrt(omega_B)P sqrt(omega_B)`; there is no simultaneous-decoder
requirement. Inside the disk, a pure product state with Bob vector
`(x,sqrt(1-x^2-z^2),z)` and A_0=A_1=1 attains zero.
This proves the first part of (3), including its boundaries.

### 2.2 Arbitrary dimensions and one universal qutrit pair

Take any pure decomposition of omega_AB in (1). Each component has Schmidt
rank at most two because B is a qubit. If its two signed correlations are
x_k,z_k, its entanglement is at least gamma(|x_k|,|z_k|), by the preceding
bound on its Schmidt support; compressed observables remain contractions.
Since C<=gamma, and C is convex and coordinatewise nondecreasing,

$$
\sum_k p_k S(\omega_B^k)
\ge\sum_k p_k C(|x_k|,|z_k|)
\ge C\!\left(\sum_k p_k|x_k|,\sum_k p_k|z_k|\right)
\ge C(x,z).
\tag{6}
$$

Infimize over pure decompositions. This proves E_d>=C for every d and
also handles negative component correlations. No Jordan decomposition,
shared Bob flag or bound on d enters the converse.

For the upper bound use the evaluated two-atom optimizer of C. Every
nonclassical profile can be written as a mixture of one compatible
boundary point (u,s), u^2+s^2=1, and one exact-axis atom, say (1,v), with

$$
(x,z)=(1-p)(u,s)+p(1,v),\qquad C(x,z)=p f(v).
\tag{7}
$$

In the central region take v=1 and p=w(x,z); the supporting point is
`((x-p)/(1-p),(z-p)/(1-p))`. In the strict-saving region the unique
scalar root in the profile note supplies v,u,s,p. Exact axes and the
corner use p=1; use the exchanged construction if z>x.

Embed a classical state |c> in the one-dimensional block of (4), and choose
Bob's pure |beta> with Bloch vector (u,0,s). On the other block put

$$
|\psi_v^X\rangle
=\sqrt\lambda\,|0\rangle_A|+\rangle_{X,B}
 +\sqrt{1-\lambda}\,|1\rangle_A|-\rangle_{X,B},
\qquad \lambda=(1+\sqrt{1-v^2})/2.
$$

It has entropy f(v) and correlations (1,v) for (4). Thus the qutrit-qubit
state

$$
\omega=(1-p)|c\rangle\langle c|\otimes|\beta\rangle\langle\beta|
       +p|\psi_v^X\rangle\langle\psi_v^X|
\tag{8}
$$

has the desired correlations. Its displayed decomposition gives E_F<=p f(v),
and (6) gives the reverse bound. Equivalently, measuring A's orthogonal
flag gives the usual locally flagged formation identity. Bob has no flag.
For the other exact axis use Schmidt bases A_X and B_Z; the same fixed
observables (4) then give (v,1). On the disk the one-dimensional block
alone suffices. This proves fixed-pair qutrit attainment and all d>=3 by
embedding.

These qutrit states realize the two specified correlations. They are not
asserted to realize the entire unbiased noisy-Pauli assemblage on that
same dimension. The full assemblage's cost also equals C, by the earlier
orbit proof; forgetting its other data happens not to reduce the minimum
formation cost, although it can reduce realization dimension.

### 2.3 Strictness and the exact minimum dimension

Along any fixed ray outside the disk, the raw entropy
`g(r)=h_2((1-sqrt(2-r^2))/2)`, 1<r<sqrt(2), is strictly concave.
For completeness, putting y=sqrt(2-r^2) gives the sign expression

$$
g''(r)=\frac{2\operatorname{atanh}y-y(2-y^2)/(1-y^2)}{y^3\ln2}<0.
$$

The numerator vanishes at y=0 and has derivative
`-y^2(1+y^2)/(1-y^2)^2<0`. Hence a point with both coordinates below one
and outside the disk lies strictly above the chord between its ray's
unit-circle point and exact-axis endpoint. That chord is an admissible
C construction, so gamma>C. On the disk both vanish; on the exact axes
both equal f. Equation (5) follows. A dimension-one realization cannot
leave the disk, while dimension two attains the positive exact-axis
costs. Together with (3), this proves the minimum-dimension statement.

## 3. Three benchmarks with different assumptions

Let G be the minimum E_F for two qubits with *fixed orthogonal* Pauli
readouts on both sides and those same two correlations. Bilateral Pauli
twirling preserves the data and cannot increase E_F; Wootters' formula
then gives

$$
G(x,z)=f([x+z-1]_+).
\tag{9}
$$

This is the same evaluated function as the fixed-output-channel comparison
in [the earlier audit](TENSOR_FORMATION_AND_CHANNEL_COMPARISON.md), Section 5.
That proof's Bell weights attain the minimum even with maximally mixed
marginals, so allowing arbitrary marginals here changes no value.

| Correlation-realization assumptions | Exact minimum E_F |
|---|---|
| Both systems qubits, fixed orthogonal readouts on both | G |
| Both systems qubits, arbitrary binary readouts on A | gamma |
| Trusted qubit B; A dimension at least three, arbitrary readouts | C |
| Trusted qubit B; one fixed qutrit pair (4) | C |

The whole ordering is C<=gamma<=G. If x+z<=1 all three vanish. If
x+z>1 but x^2+z^2<=1, then C=gamma=0<G. Outside the disk with both
coordinates below one,

$$
C<\gamma<G,
$$

where the second inequality follows from
`(x+z-1)^2-(x^2+z^2-1)=2(1-x)(1-z)>0` and strict increase of f.
On either exact axis all three equal f of the other coordinate.
These complete equality sets make the prior-resource comparisons explicit.

## 4. All-data Bell methods do not remove the trust distinction

The complete probability table obtained from the noisy-Pauli assemblage
and Bob's X/Z measurements is

$$
P(a,b\mid i,j)=\frac{1+ab\,\delta_{ij}c_i}{4},
\quad c_X=x,\quad c_Z=z.
\tag{10}
$$

It is Bell local throughout the entire square. Let shared independent
uniform signs U,V determine Alice's outputs for X,Z. Bob outputs U N_X
or V N_Z, where independent local noise signs have means x,z. All
marginals and cross correlations vanish, and the two matching correlations
are exactly x,z. This reproduces (10) and has a finite-dimensional
separable quantum implementation with classical registers.

Therefore every sound unrestricted-dimension fully device-independent
entanglement certificate based only on this entire table gives zero.
It cannot evaluate positive C outside the disk. The missing input is
Bob's trusted qubit and anticommuting operators. Additional rotated Bob
measurements give a larger data set and are not covered by this argument;
the earlier auxiliary-CHSH comparison used precisely that trusted structure.
The local-model argument is an elementary model distinction, not a novelty.

## 6. The unrestricted optimality attempt: exact outcomes

The original unresolved all-state gate remains

$$
\sum_{i,b}\|\sqrt\rho P_{i,b}\sqrt\rho\|_1
\le\sqrt2\,n+(2-\sqrt2)S(\rho).
\tag{11}
$$

Neither (3) nor a sum of single-site formation costs proves (11): the
same retained system serves all local queries, and the required sharp
monogamy/tensorization statement is not supplied. Two attempted global
routes were tested here and have exact counterexamples to their proposed
relaxations, recorded in
[ENTROPY_PROOF_RELAXATIONS.md](ENTROPY_PROOF_RELAXATIONS.md):

- Keeping each exact basis prior and only the total Holevo information
  budget permits too much Hamming decoding, even at every nontrivial
  subset equality seed. Signal-overlap or conditional constraints matter.
- The complete rank-two spectral envelope, norm four, and zero partial
  traces do not imply the all-state entropy-energy bound. An explicit
  Hamiltonian satisfies all these relaxed conditions and violates that
  bound, but contains forbidden trusted Pauli terms. It is not an
  admissible interface counterexample.

No all-state proof, admissible violating seed or unrestricted optimum
was obtained. These obstructions identify information a future proof must
retain; they are not further claims that testing restricted states proves
the conjecture.
