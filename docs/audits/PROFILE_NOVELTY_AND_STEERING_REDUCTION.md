## 2. Cope's two-parameter plot is not this two-noise family

The family used in Cope's p. 6 calculation fixes the first measurement:

$$
\tau_{s|0}=\frac{I+sZ}{4},\qquad
\tau_{s|1}=\frac{I+s(uX+wZ)}4
$$

on its real unbiased slice. Both parameters describe the second
measurement. In our interior `0<x,z<1`, all four outcomes have full rank:

$$
\det\sigma^{x,z}_{s|X}=\frac{1-x^2}{16}>0,\qquad
\det\sigma^{x,z}_{s|Z}=\frac{1-z^2}{16}>0.
$$

The first pair in Cope's family instead has determinant zero. Outcome
ranks survive trusted-side unitaries, invertible single-Kraus filters and
setting/outcome relabels. Thus these transformations cannot identify the
families. A singular qubit filter gives a rank-deficient marginal, not
`I/2`. Adding outcome noise can change ranks, but only supplies a forward
construction; it does not preserve optimal cost reversibly.

There is a second elementary distinction: our zero-cost region has
two-dimensional interior, whereas the exact-first real family is compatible
only on `u=0`. Indeed compatibility with a sharp Z measurement requires
the other effects to commute with Z. No invertible affine change of the
two parameters identifies their zero-cost sets.

**Additional prior overlap:** Cope's Fig. 4 already reports mixtures of an
unsteerable assemblage and a partially entangled assemblage that improve
both equal-entropy and steering-weight constructions. The idea of such
mixing, or of a non-flat entropy roof, is not our novelty. The remaining
candidate is the complete analytic value and matching support proof for
the independently noisy orthogonal family, including its phase boundary
and the specified fixed-cap operational result.

These rank and parameter arguments exclude the stated direct equivalences.
They do not rule out a more elaborate reduction from another prior theorem.

## 3. Exact separation from the whole inspected weighted-CHSH family

Put `r=sqrt(x^2+z^2)` and

$$
L_{\rm rad}(x,z)=\left[\frac{r-1}{\sqrt2-1}\right]_+.
$$

**Proposition 1.** Optimizing the affine formation certificate in
Zhu–Zhang–Ma Theorem 2 over every `alpha>=1`, both assignments of the
assemblage settings, every trusted binary measurement, and either Bell-party
orientation yields exactly `L_rad`. Furthermore,

$$
\boxed{C(x,z)>L_{\rm rad}(x,z)
\quad\text{whenever }x\ne z\text{ and }x^2+z^2>1.}
\tag{1}
$$

This is a supplied deduction from the printed certificate, not a theorem
attributed to that paper.

Their convention and bound are

$$
S_\alpha=\alpha\langle A_0(B_0+B_1)\rangle
             +\langle A_1(B_0-B_1)\rangle,
\qquad
E_F\ge\frac{S_\alpha-2\alpha}{2\sqrt{1+\alpha^2}-2\alpha}.
\tag{2}
$$

For any realization of our assemblage, the two trusted difference
operators are `xX/2,zZ/2`. Trace-norm duality, allowing arbitrary Hermitian
contractions `B_0,B_1`, gives

$$
\max S_\alpha=2\sqrt{\alpha^2x^2+z^2}.
$$

The independent maximizers test `(alpha xX+zZ)/2` and
`(alpha xX-zZ)/2`. Scalar parts of the trusted observables do not contribute.
For `r<=1` this value is at most `2alpha`, so its positive part is zero.
For `1<=r<=sqrt(2)`,

$$
\sqrt{\alpha^2x^2+z^2}
\le\sqrt{\alpha^2+r^2-1}
\le\alpha+(\sqrt{\alpha^2+1}-\alpha)
             \frac{r-1}{\sqrt2-1}.
\tag{3}
$$

The first inequality uses `x<=1`. The second is the endpoint chord bound
for the convex function `h(r)=sqrt(alpha^2+r^2-1)`:

$$
h''(r)=\frac{\alpha^2-1}{(\alpha^2+r^2-1)^{3/2}}\ge0.
$$

Substitution into (2) bounds every certificate by `L_rad`; `alpha=1`
attains it. Interchanging x and z changes nothing in the argument.

If the Bell parties are interchanged, the optimized Bell value is
`(alpha+1)r`. The resulting affine function of r is nonpositive at `r=1`
and at most one at `r=sqrt(2)`, since
`(alpha+1)sqrt(2)<=2sqrt(alpha^2+1)`. It too lies below the same chord.

To prove strictness, use the raw convex function
`ell(y)=(||y||_2-1)/(sqrt(2)-1)`. Every generator of C has cost at least
ell. Equality for a free generator requires the unit circle. For an
exact-axis generator `(1,v,f(v))`, `0<v<1`, strict radial entropy
concavity, proved in the profile note, gives

$$
f(v)>\frac{\sqrt{1+v^2}-1}{\sqrt2-1}.
$$

Thus the only positive-cost equality generator is `(1,1,1)`. A minimizing
finite mixture exists. If `C=ell` at a target outside the disk, every
positive-weight atom must attain its individual inequality, and equality
in the Euclidean triangle inequality forces all its profile vectors onto
the same ray. Positive cost requires `(1,1,1)` to occur, so the target is
symmetric. Coordinate weakening cannot preserve the norm unless it has
zero slack. Conversely C and `L_rad` agree on the disk and the symmetric
segment by the established formula. This proves the exact equality set.

For orientation, at `(.99,.5)` the radial value is approximately `.263388`,
whereas the certified C is approximately `.324848`. Equation (1) proves
the separation throughout the asymmetric region without numerical evidence.

This result concerns pointwise optimization of the printed affine
certificates. It does not exclude stronger deductions retaining several
correlation constraints jointly, other Bell inequalities, or other
one-sided entanglement bounds. The qubit-only formulas in the same paper
cannot be applied directly to arbitrary untrusted classical flags.

## 4. Why linear-entropy optimization does not already give C

Tóth–Moroder–Gühne supply genuine prior machinery for this type of task:
their supplement's steering program bounds the convex roof of
`1-Tr(rho_B^2)`, and includes an equally noisy Pauli example. It is a
different objective from marginal von Neumann entropy.

Here is a precise obstruction to converting its scalar answer into C.
For a pure state with qubit marginal let `t=2(1-Tr(rho_B^2))`. Its
entanglement entropy is

$$
g(t)=f(\sqrt t),\qquad 0\le t\le1.
$$

Writing `s=sqrt(1-t)` gives

$$
g''(t)=-\frac{s/(1-s^2)-\operatorname{atanh}s}
                   {4s^3\ln2}<0.
$$

Therefore `g(t)>t` in the interior, and taking convex roofs gives the
valid universal conversion `E_F>=2E_lin`. This scalar conversion is sharp
when arbitrary flags on the untrusted side are permitted: a locally
flagged mixture of a Bell state with probability p and a product state
otherwise has `E_F=p` and `E_lin=p/2`. The upper bounds follow from that
decomposition; measuring the flag and average monotonicity give matching
lower bounds. Hence replacing the conversion by `g(2E_lin)` is invalid.

At the exact-axis target `(1,z)`, the known pure-seed orbit construction
has linear formation cost `z^2/2`. The minimum compatible-state linear
cost is consequently at most this. Even its exact value, followed only
by the best universal scalar conversion, yields at most `z^2`, whereas
the entropy optimum is `f(z)>z^2` for `0<z<1`. Additional profile-aware
analysis is necessary. This does not preclude adapting the prior general
methods to a new objective; it distinguishes their printed result from
the entropy evaluation claimed here.

The 2026 Lee–Lin–Miranowicz–Chen paper likewise evaluates a different
quantity: its Appendix F Pauli witness is `[eta^2-1/2]_+`, after replacing
its noise parameter by `1-eta`. Result 3 concerns pure-state entanglement.
Neither statement is an exact mixed-realization `E_FA` formula. Arbitrarily
rescaling that witness is not a proved formation-entropy conversion.

## 5. Exact steering formulation of unrestricted equal accuracy

Let `d=2^n`, `j=(i,b)`, `b in {X,Z}`. On the trusted n-qubit reference
define the **local-query assemblage**

$$
\mathcal L^\eta_{n;s|j}=\frac{I+s\eta P_j}{2d},\qquad
A_n(\eta)=E_{FA}(\mathcal L_n^\eta).
$$

It has `2n` binary settings. The untrusted system is the retained register
and branch flag. Formation entropy is an auxiliary resource here; the
physical worst-case dimension remains the original resource.

**Proposition 2.** With the repository's normalized score

$$
f_n(\rho)=\frac1{2n}\sum_j\|\sqrt\rho P_j\sqrt\rho\|_1,
$$

one has

$$
\boxed{A_n(\eta)=\inf_{\sum_a p_af_n(\rho_a)\ge\eta}
                         \sum_a p_aS(\rho_a),\qquad
R(\eta)=\inf_n\frac{A_n(\eta)}n
       =\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{A_n(\eta)}n.}
\tag{4}
$$

The ensemble in (4) is finite. Individual components need not meet eta.

For the first direction, decompose `L_n^eta=sum_a p_a tau^a`, and let
`rho_a=sum_s tau^a_{s|j}`. The marginals average to `I/d`. Define

$$
K_a=\sqrt{dp_a}\sqrt{\rho_a^T},\qquad
N^a_{s|j}=(\rho_a^{-1/2}\tau^a_{s|j}\rho_a^{-1/2})^T.
$$

Use support inverses and complete the POVM on each kernel. These obey

$$
\sum_aK_a^\dagger K_a=I,\qquad
\sum_aK_a^\dagger N^a_{s|j}K_a
=d(\mathcal L^\eta_{n;s|j})^T
=\frac{I+s\eta P_j}{2}.
$$

All queries are real. Their correlation coefficients and trace-norm
duality give `sum_a p_a f_n(rho_a)>=eta`; the normalized Gram entropy
is `S(rho_a)`. Thus the seed infimum is no larger than `A_n`.

Conversely, complete each seed `sqrt(rho_a)` with its full Pauli orbit
and separately optimal binary decoders. Site permutations and independent
local Hadamards act transitively on the `2n` queries. The proved character
projection therefore gives exact common contrast `f_n(rho_a)`, with all
branch Gram entropies unchanged. Mix the complete instruments with weights
`p_a`, and reduce excess contrast by output flips. The reverse
instrument-to-assemblage map yields the desired decomposition and entropy.
Every outcome is kept; there is no postselection. Allowing nonextremal
components leaves the infimum unchanged, because extremal refinement
cannot increase average marginal entropy.

For the rate statement use the already proved
[entropy characterization](../ENTROPY_RATE_CHARACTERIZATION.md):
`R=E=inf_n e_n/n=lim_n e_n/n`, with E convex and nondecreasing. Every
feasible ensemble satisfies

$$
E(\eta)\le E\!\left(\sum_a p_af_n(\rho_a)\right)
\le\sum_a p_aE(f_n(\rho_a))
\le\frac1n\sum_a p_aS(\rho_a).
$$

A single-state ensemble also gives `A_n<=e_n`. Consequently
`nE<=A_n<=e_n`; taking infima and squeezing the limit proves (4).
No continuity of general `E_FA` is assumed. The fixed-cap interpretation
comes from the existing compression-and-completion theorem, not from
renaming an average entropy as physical memory.

## 6. The precise missing identity, and a possible route to refutation

Put `sigma^eta=L_1^eta`, and define

$$
C_\eta=C(\eta,\eta)
=\left[\frac{\eta-1/\sqrt2}{1-1/\sqrt2}\right]_+,
\qquad
B_n(\eta)=E_{FA}((\sigma^\eta)^{\otimes n}).
$$

The full tensor product in B has every setting tuple `(b_1,...,b_n)`
and every outcome tuple `(s_1,...,s_n)`. For each retained query, fix
arbitrary settings at the other sites and marginalize their outcomes.
This gives the local-query assemblage, with the trusted marginal unchanged.
Apply this map to any decomposition; refine if needed. Product
decompositions supply the last inequality in

$$
\boxed{nR(\eta)\le A_n(\eta)\le B_n(\eta)\le nC_\eta.}
\tag{5}
$$

The unrestricted subset-rate conjecture is therefore equivalent to
`A_n(eta)=nC_eta` for every n and eta. It would imply ordinary tensor-power
formation additivity `B_n=nC_eta` on this symmetric family. Conversely,
**any certified strict inequality `B_n<nC_eta` would refute unrestricted
optimality** at that contrast and, by the established rate dichotomy, give
advantage at every interior contrast. No such inequality is established
here.

Proving only `B_n=nC_eta` would not settle the problem: the intervening
inequality `A_n<=B_n` could be strict. Likewise neither ordinary tensor-copy
additivity nor a theorem about another steering measure can be silently
substituted for the needed local-query identity. Cope–Uola Eq. (22) also
retains smoothing and two bounding regularizations; it does not identify
(4) with their assemblage entanglement cost.

## 7. Commuting input queries need not have jointly optimal decoders

Here is a concrete obstruction to assuming a tuple extension for every
optimized seed branch. It illustrates a standard compression phenomenon;
it is not presented as a new incompatibility principle.

On an output qubit set

$$
G_{r,t}=\frac{I+(rX+tZ)/\sqrt2}{4},\qquad r,t\in\{-1,+1\}.
$$

These rank-one effects sum to I. Choose real row factors forming an
isometry `V:C^2 -> C^4` with
`V^dagger |r,t><r,t| V=G_{r,t}`. Label the two-qubit computational basis
so that `Z_1|r,t>=r|r,t>` and `Z_2|r,t>=t|r,t>`, and put
`L=V^dagger/sqrt(2)`. Then

$$
\operatorname{Tr}L^\dagger L=1,\qquad LL^\dagger=I/2,
\qquad
LZ_1L^\dagger=\frac{X}{2\sqrt2},\qquad
LZ_2L^\dagger=\frac{Z}{2\sqrt2}.
\tag{6}
$$

The last two equalities follow by summing `rG_{r,t}` and `tG_{r,t}`.
The sandwiched queries are invertible, so equality in trace-norm duality
uniquely requires decoders X and Z. These sharp measurements have no joint
POVM: every joint positive effect dominated by an X eigenprojector is
supported on that eigenspace, forcing every second marginal to commute
with X, whereas a Z eigenprojector does not.

Equivalently the normalized seed produces two assemblage settings

$$
\tau_{s|1}=V(I+sX)V^\dagger/4,\qquad
\tau_{s|2}=V(I+sZ)V^\dagger/4,
$$

with common marginal `VV^dagger/2`. Any positive joint extension would
be supported in `ran V`; conjugating by `V^dagger` and multiplying by two
would give the impossible joint POVM for X and Z.

This example shows that arbitrary decomposition components cannot
automatically be extended to tuple outcomes at their original cost.
It does **not** show that the target `L_n^eta` lacks a tuple extension:
its product extension is explicit. It proves neither `A_n<B_n` nor a
violation of the sharp entropy inequality. Kaur–Wang–Wilde Proposition 11
requires a joint no-signalling assemblage and concerns intrinsic
steerability, so it supplies neither of those missing conclusions.

## 8. Publication judgment and verification

The contribution can now be distinguished concretely from the inspected
closest sources: a complete von Neumann formation-entropy value for two
independently noisy orthogonal qubit observables, with a matching optimizer
and a worst-case memory-rate theorem for a stated collective comparison
class. The resource definition, compatible/entangled mixing mechanism,
exact-axis atom, symmetric slice and general convex-roof methods are prior.

This supports a focused publication case; it does not certify that the
formula has never appeared elsewhere. The source search was bounded and
the comparisons concern the exact versions and theorem families listed.
No claim of a new resource, unrestricted optimum, or broad collective
equal-accuracy advantage is justified. A paper should state its restriction
in the main theorem, not defer it to a caveat.

The weighted-family optimization and its strict separation were independently
reconstructed within this workspace. The local-query reduction, normalization,
Jensen step, tuple marginalization and decoder obstruction received a
separate independent proof review. These are internal checks, not external
peer review. The prior rational certificate was not rerun: neither its
implementation nor the formula it evaluates changed. No matrix search or
large simulation was used. An optional SymPy identity check could not run
because SymPy was absent; the explicit identities in (6) were checked
algebraically and are not credited as a computational test.

The original commit-pinned audit and LICENSE remain unchanged. The new
identities refine the research question; they do not close it.
