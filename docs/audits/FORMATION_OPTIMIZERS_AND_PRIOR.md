### 1.1 Its complete concurrence family evaluates w

Use `c(omega)` for convex-roof concurrence, with pure-state value
`2 sqrt(det omega_B)`, to distinguish it from the project's entropy cost C.
Let K(x,z) be its least value among all finite-dimensional realizations of
the two data. For weights a,b>=0 put R=sqrt(a^2+b^2) and T=a+b>0.
The source's distribution with probabilities a/T,b/T on trusted X,Z has
separable guessing bias R/T. Theorem 2, with guessing probability equal
to one half plus one half of the bias, gives

$$
ax+bz\le (1-c)R+cT.
\tag{1}
$$

Taking all nonnegative weights characterizes the downward-closed convex
region generated by `(1-q)` times the positive unit disk and q times
(1,1). The minimum q for a target outside the disk solves

$$
(x-q)^2+(z-q)^2=(1-q)^2.
$$

The smaller root lies in `[0,min(x,z)]`; within the disk the minimum is
zero. Consequently

$$
K(x,z)=w(x,z)
=\left[x+z-1-\sqrt{2(1-x)(1-z)}\right]_+.
\tag{2}
$$

For clarity, (1) gives the lower bound by separation. The product-plus-Bell
flagged state with q=w and compatible profile
`((x-q)/(1-q),(z-q)/(1-q))` attains it. The Bell corner uses q=1.
Thus (2) is a direct application of the complete prior weighted family,
not a new evaluation of w or a formula claimed printed in that source.

Write `f(v)=h_2((1-sqrt(1-v^2))/2)`. For every finite-dimensional state
with a qubit B, convexity of f and its pure-state identity give

$$
E_F(\omega)\ge f(c(\omega))\ge f(w(x,z)).
\tag{3}
$$

The equality `E_F=f(c)` for two qubits cannot simply be imported into
higher dimensions. In fact the entire two-data entropy profile obeys

$$
C(x,z)>f(w(x,z))
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
x^2+z^2>1,\quad \max(x,z)<1.
\tag{4}
$$

To prove strictness, use the evaluated optimizer of C: a weight p of an
exact-axis atom with concurrence v and entropy f(v), and a compatible
atom of weight 1-p. In the stated region, 0<p<1 and v>0, so its flagged
state has concurrence pv and C=p f(v). Equation (2) gives w<=pv, and
strict convexity of f gives

$$
C=p f(v)>f(pv)\ge f(w).
$$

On the disk both vanish. On an exact axis w is the other coordinate
and C=f(w). This proves the complete equality set. The prior concurrence
tradeoff is exact for its resource but a scalar conversion of that answer
does not evaluate the entropy resource in the noisy nonclassical interior.

### 1.2 The pure weighted geometry already supplies the starting optimization

For a>=b>=0, a>0 and pure concurrence c, the prior pure-state support is

$$
B_c(a,b)=\max_{u^2+s^2=1}
\left[a\sqrt{c^2+(1-c^2)u^2}
+b\sqrt{c^2+(1-c^2)s^2}\right].
$$

Its two square roots have squared sum 1+c^2 and each lies in [c,1].
Cauchy–Schwarz, followed by the box constraint, therefore evaluates it as

$$
B_c(a,b)=
\begin{cases}
\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\sqrt{1+c^2},&0\le c\le b/a,\\
a+bc,&b/a\le c\le1.
\end{cases}
\tag{5}
$$

This piecewise evaluation is supplied here from the prior optimization.
The entropy-penalized support is `M(a,b)=max_c[B_c(a,b)-f(c)]`.
On the first branch set r=sqrt(1+c^2). The already proved strict radial
concavity of `f(sqrt(r^2-1))` makes its negative plus Rr convex, so the
maximum is at c=0 or c=b/a. The latter is an exact-axis contact.
Every exact-axis atom is feasible. Hence

$$
M(a,b)=\max\{\sqrt{a^2+b^2},\ a+f^*(b)\},
\qquad f^*(b)=\max_{0\le v\le1}[bv-f(v)].
\tag{6}
$$

This is the existing profile dual, recovered from a close prior theorem.
The entropy reweighting, joint convexification, evaluated phase boundary
and optimizer remain the specific supplied calculations. The generic
weighted guessing geometry and convex-roof machinery are established.
This is a stronger overlap finding than merely comparing unrelated tasks;
it narrows the originality case without proving complete subsumption.

## 2. Optimal entropy realizations must separate classical and entangled sectors

The earlier construction showed orthogonal flags suffice. Here we prove
that every minimum-entropy realization has such a separation in the noisy
nonclassical interior. It is an exact equality theorem, not a robustness
estimate or a self-testing claim from measured data alone.

Assume x>=z, x^2+z^2>1, and x<1. Let the evaluated optimizer parameters
be p,u,s,v, so

$$
(x,z)=(1-p)(u,s)+p(1,v),\qquad
u^2+s^2=1,\quad C=p f(v),\quad0<p<1.
\tag{7}
$$

In the central phase v=1; in the strict-saving phase 0<v<1. All of u,s
are positive. The parameters come from the
[profile theorem](../PRODUCT_DIAGONAL_PROFILE_RATE.md), Section 5.1.
Exchange X/Z and their Alice observables when z>x.

**Theorem.** If a state omega and contractions A_0,A_1 realize (x,z) with
`E_F(omega)=C(x,z)`, its occupied Alice space separates orthogonally into:

- Classical sectors labelled by signs epsilon,delta in {+1,-1}, where
  A_0=epsilon I and A_1=delta I and Bob is the pure state with Bloch
  vector `(epsilon u,0,delta s)`.
- An entangled sector on which A_0 and A_1 are anticommuting reflections.
  This sector carries total probability p. Each component in an attaining
  formation decomposition has concurrence v and exact-X correlation one.

The state has no coherence between these sectors. In particular, every
qutrit optimum is, up to an Alice unitary, one of the following states:

$$
A_0=\epsilon\oplus Z_A,\qquad A_1=\delta\oplus X_A,
\qquad
\omega=(1-p)|c\rangle\langle c|\otimes|\beta_{\epsilon\delta}\rangle
\langle\beta_{\epsilon\delta}|\ \oplus\ p\tau_{v,m},
\tag{8}
$$

where, in the ordered basis `|0>_A|+>_B, |1>_A|->_B`,

$$
\tau_{v,m}=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1+m&v\\v&1-m\end{pmatrix},
\qquad |m|\le\sqrt{1-v^2}.
\tag{9}
$$

All these states attain C. On the central phase v=1 the entangled block
is uniquely a Bell state. On the strict-saving phase the allowed m
interval records genuine nonuniqueness, including mixed entangled blocks.
For the fixed universal pair `1⊕Z,1⊕X`, only epsilon=delta=+1 occurs.
No uniqueness of the physical multi-input interface encoder is asserted.

The block family (9) itself is established: after local basis changes it
is Verstraete–Wolf [quant-ph/0112012v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0112012v1),
Eq. (4), p. 2, with their parameter a=-m and concurrence v. The added
deduction here is the necessity of the displayed flagged form for every
optimal qutrit realization of the joint data, not a new state family.

### 2.1 Equality forces the two pure-component types

At the target, C has a supporting plane `cost>=a x+b z-R` with a,b>0.
In the strict-saving phase its contacts among the generating atoms are
exactly `(u,s,0)` and `(1,v,f(v))`, by the earlier tangent-plane proof.
In the central phase, take R=a+b-1 and `(u,s)=(a/R,b/R)`. Differentiating
w gives `a>=b>=f'(1)=1/ln2`. Thus `a+bt-f(t)` increases strictly up to
t=1 for t<1, and the only positive-cost generator contact is the Bell
corner. The free disk has the single supporting point (u,s).

The same plane has no other contacts with the raw pure-state entropy
gamma: strictly outside the disk with both coordinates below one,
gamma>C; on the disk and exact axes use the contacts just identified.
Take an attaining finite pure-state decomposition of omega, which exists
in finite dimensions. For each component its signed correlations x_k,z_k
and entanglement E_k satisfy

$$
E_k\ge\gamma(|x_k|,|z_k|)
\ge a|x_k|+b|z_k|-R
\ge ax_k+bz_k-R.
\tag{10}
$$

Their average is exactly C, so every positive-weight component saturates
all three inequalities. Positivity of a,b fixes the correlation signs.
The two contact types and their total weights in (7) are therefore forced.
This argument applies to any attaining formation decomposition.

### 2.2 Saturated contractions force orthogonal reducing supports

A compatible component has E_k=0, hence is a pure product. Its Bob Bloch
vector must saturate the supporting disk bound. Thus it has the form
`(epsilon u,0,delta s)`, and its Alice vector a obeys
`A_0 a=epsilon a`, `A_1 a=delta a`. This follows from equality in the
expectation bound for a Hermitian contraction, since u,s>0. Keeping the
four possible signs is essential; positive correlations alone do not fix
Bob's marginal signs.

For an entangled component, correlation one against X implies

$$
(A_0\otimes I)|\psi\rangle=(I\otimes X)|\psi\rangle.
$$

Hermiticity on its Schmidt support forces the Bob marginal to commute
with X. Entropy f(v) then fixes its eigenvalues to
`(1±sqrt(1-v^2))/2`. With a suitable orthonormal Alice pair e_+,e_-, write

$$
|\psi\rangle=\sqrt\lambda\,|e_+\rangle|+\rangle
+\sqrt{1-\lambda}\,|e_-\rangle|-\rangle,
\quad A_0e_\pm=\pm e_\pm.
$$

The Z correlation equals
`v Re<e_+|A_1|e_->=v`, and v>0. Contractivity and equality therefore
force `A_1 e_+=e_-`, `A_1 e_-=e_+`. This two-dimensional support is
reducing for both observables, with anticommuting Pauli restrictions.
The span of all entangled supports remains reducing and satisfies the
same anticommutation relation.

On a classical joint-eigenvector the anticommutator has eigenvalue
2 epsilon delta; on the entangled span it is zero. Since the
anticommutator is Hermitian, these spaces are orthogonal. Distinct
classical sign sectors are also orthogonal. The attaining pure
decomposition consequently has no cross-sector coherence, proving the
first part of the theorem.

### 2.3 Completing the qutrit classification

Both weights in (7) are positive. In dimension three the classical
support therefore has dimension one and the entangled support dimension
two. Only one sign sector is occupied. On the latter, choose an Alice
basis so A_0=Z and A_1=X. Every entangled component has the same
off-diagonal entry v/2 and diagonal difference
`±sqrt(1-v^2)` in the displayed correlated basis. Their mixtures are
exactly (9). Conversely, (9) is the convex mixture of those two pure
endpoints with concurrence v and entropy f(v). The state (8) therefore
has an explicit decomposition of average entropy p f(v), and the general
C lower bound proves equality. This proves necessity and sufficiency.

### 2.4 A limited consequence for the two-input monogamy attempt

For a pure state on A B_1 B_2 with both B_i qubits, suppose the first
marginal has a noisy nonclassical profile and `E_F(A:B_1)=C_1`. Its
rank is at most two. The forced orthogonal separation therefore consists
of one pure product component and one pure entangled component. Their
weights are 1-p,p, giving `S(A)=h_2(p)+C_1` and `S(B_2)=h_2(p)`.
Since `C_2<=E_F(A:B_2)<=S(B_2)`, the desired sum `C_1+C_2<=S(A)`
holds in this equality case. General marginals need not saturate C;
the conclusion is not an unrestricted monogamy theorem.

## 3. An exact matrix certificate for the unresolved two-input joint bound

The joint score j_2 from the
[earlier audit](ROOF_PROVENANCE_AND_JOINT_SCORE.md) is at most the local
score f_2. Its conjectured entropy bound is weaker than the original
local-query bound. The following equivalent form retains the shared
state and all four context constraints. It is a standard SDP/entropy
duality application, not a claim that the bound has been proved.

Set `t=1/sqrt(2)` and `a=(1-t)/2`. For each context
`b=(b_1,b_2) in {X,Z}^2` and pair of signs s, define

$$
D_{s|b}=\frac{s_1P_{1,b_1}+s_2P_{2,b_2}}2,\qquad
J_b(\rho)=\max_{\substack{\Gamma_{s|b}\succeq0\\
\sum_s\Gamma_{s|b}=\rho}}\sum_s\operatorname{Tr}(D_{s|b}\Gamma_{s|b}),
\quad j_2=\frac14\sum_bJ_b.
\tag{11}
$$

**Equivalent certificate.** The inequality `j_2(rho)<=t+a S(rho)`
holds for every two-qubit density matrix if and only if, for every
positive-definite density matrix sigma on C^4, there exist four Hermitian
matrices Y_b satisfying

$$
Y_b\succeq D_{s|b}\quad\text{for every }b,s,\qquad
\frac14\sum_bY_b\preceq tI-a\log_2\sigma.
\tag{12}
$$

For a proof, fix sigma and put K=-log_2 sigma. Maximize

$$
\frac14\sum_{b,s}\operatorname{Tr}(D_{s|b}\Gamma_{s|b})
-a\operatorname{Tr}(\rho K)
\tag{13}
$$

over the common state rho>=0 of trace one and the four positive
decompositions in (11). Its value is
`max_rho[j_2(rho)-a Tr(rho K)]`. Its dual minimizes lambda subject to

$$
Y_b\succeq D_{s|b},\qquad
\frac14\sum_bY_b\preceq\lambda I+aK.
$$

Indeed the multipliers Z_b for `rho-sum_s Gamma=0` obey
`Z_b>=D_{s|b}/4` and `sum_b Z_b<=lambda I+aK`; set Y_b=4Z_b.
The primal is strictly feasible at rho=I/4 and every Gamma=I/16.
Its feasible set is compact. The dual is feasible with scalar Y_b and
sufficiently large lambda. Strong duality gives equal finite optima and
an attained dual minimum. The singular fixed-rho dual caveat from the
earlier audit does not apply to this common-state optimization.

If the entropy bound holds, nonnegativity of relative entropy gives
`Tr(rho K)>=S(rho)`, so (13) is at most t and its attained dual supplies
(12). Conversely, (12) gives `j_2(rho)<=t-a Tr(rho log_2 sigma)`.
Infimizing over positive-definite sigma gives `t+a S(rho)`. Singular rho
is included by taking `sigma_e=(1-e)rho+e I/4` and e decreasing to zero.

In particular, infeasibility of (12) for one fixed positive-definite
sigma forces the attained primal value in (13) to exceed t. Its state
then satisfies

$$
j_2(\rho)>t+a\operatorname{Tr}(\rho K)\ge t+aS(\rho).
$$

This would be a genuine quantum violation. The positive operators
`rho^(-1/2) Gamma_{s|b} rho^(-1/2)` form context POVMs on the support
of rho; no inverse on its kernel or postselected physical success is
needed. Since f_2>=j_2, the normalized-seed orbit construction would
convert that state into an admissible local-query rate improvement.
Conversely every strict violation, including a singular one, is detected
by some positive-definite potential sigma_e as above.

Neither a universal construction of these covers nor an infeasible
instance was found. A universal cover would settle this two-input
**joint** inequality only; it would still not prove the stronger local
inequality. Unlike the relaxations refuted in PR #17, a failed cover has
a primal certificate within the original quantum state/POVM constraints.
