# Repository working rules

This is the Quantum Interface Cost / Falling Q1 project. Keep it separate
from the classical Objective-Revision-Memory project and the other fiction
research branches. Shared motivation is not permission to transfer theorems.

The user wants publishable, analytically led work, a clear conceptual story,
and a serious novelty audit; large simulations are not available. Preserve
the MIT license, copyright (c) 2026 Ruge Lin. Do not upload the source fiction,
private conversations, third-party papers, credentials, or unrelated files.

Read docs/STATUS.md and the current research note before editing. Record the
current main SHA. Check open issues and PRs for concurrent work. Use separate
research/<topic> and audit/<topic> branches. No force pushes or history rewrites.
Changes to theorem statements, assumptions, proofs, or novelty claims should
be proposed in a PR with their mathematical impact stated. The initial
bootstrap is not independent approval of any proof.

The Research Lead coordinates integration. The audit workspace follows
docs/WORKSPACE_REVIEW_BRIEF.md and must not merge its own report. Communication
is through versioned notes, issues, and PRs, not assumed cross-chat access.

Do not silently change one specimen to many copies, unknown quantum input to
a classical description, worst-case memory to average memory, unrestricted
encoders to product encoders, binary output sampling to mean estimation, or
uniform error to average-source error. No free quantum bypass or entanglement.

Every result needs a clear status: prior theorem, derived with supplied proof,
independently checked, finite computational evidence, conjecture, or unresolved
comparison. Passing scripts do not certify optimality, lower bounds, or novelty.
Use primary sources and exact theorem-level comparisons. Never fabricate an
external review, solver certificate, literature conclusion, or test run.
